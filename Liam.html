Personality

Liam is reminiscent of older internet culture, being highly fascinated by the spaces and media of that era and basing his interests off of it. He is especially interested in the Warrior Cats series and likes to imagine him and his siblings as such cats, dubbing himself, Kyle, and Anna as "Coaltuft", "Dualpaw", and "Wisppaw". He loves to shapeshift very frequently, and will often mimic what he is watching. He also tends to tease others.

He is still relatively new and inexperienced to shapeshifting, thus he is unaware of the full extent of his abilities and can often forget he has the option to do such.

Characteristics

Liam's core body is defined by a set of stand-out characteristics like his fur and color, however the rest of him is extremely malleable interpretation-wise. He has the power to shapeshift between different styles and representations of himself, which is an instantaneous and mostly inaudible process. He also has the ability to play into tropes extremely hard, and subvert death at his choosing.

His handwriting is frantic but not unintelligible.

Art style

Unlike other cags, you do not need to follow the strict ontology which governs them, and are free to apply whatever design characteristics you wish for him to have, so long as he is still recognizably "Liam".
