Melamine Resin ((C3H6N6 · CH2O)n, Melamine Formaldehyde, Melamine) is a polymer resin made by reacting Melamine and Formaldehyde together. Being a polymer, it is as long as it wants to be. Its cheek tufts are a similar shape to Melamine's, and it wears an orange ring—an attribute inspired by I1 Temporary Bliss State's design of Formaldehyde.[1] Its eyes also have orange pupils. The design across its body can vary, but it is typically decorated with a floral pattern.

Personality

It has mastered a variety of board games, with keen proficiency in games with tiles made from it such as Mahjong and Dominoes. When playing competitively, it frequents playing mind games on its competitors, often hinging on the tiles being made of it. It loves to pester and challenge Nihonium at Mahjong. It gambles, and is infamous for being notoriously hard to beat, resorting to illegal gambling as a result.[2]

It can usually be seen decorating itself prior to the occasion, especially common prior to playing board games and when being observed by the scientists.[2]
