Transcription

The reference sheet for aa, a semiliquid omnipedal cag. His overall body is made of a teal liquid-like substance that has glowing swirls of tealish-green throughout it. His "fur" droops down at the cheeks, and is puffed below the neck and at the tip of the tail. His paws are spherical, and his eyes are usually blank white.
