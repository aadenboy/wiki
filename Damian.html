Damian is a beige-colored bipedal cag and the informal mascot of the Aadenboy Wiki. His fur pattern is abundant in darker spots, and his ears are taller than most. He wears a neon green safety vest and seldom a hard hat.

Personality

Damian is a well-intentioned and down-to-earth person, working as an independent contractor in the carpentry field. Outside of his profession, he enjoys trying to piece together The Lore and the characteristics of the other characters. He's able to be on relatively good terms with most people and can easily adapt to their own likes and preferences, however can as easily become intimidated or worrisome.

He has access to a variety of private workstations for practices like carpentry or masonry. He begrudgingly allows Xavier to use them under his direct supervision.

Characteristics

He is able to efficiently work in any blue-collar job without prior training and possesses an uncanny ability to avoid oversight or harm to himself in such professions (to an extent). He also inexplicably has access to a Linux desktop at all times, which he operates mentally, despite the desktop not physically existing or even being connected to his mind or body in any way.

His speech bubble is the simple box-and-stick model. His speech bubble is always rectangular in form, as the contents of the speech bubble may contain any wikitext he chooses to display, regardless of whether it can be verbally said. He typically chooses to only use plain text and add a signature (shown above).
