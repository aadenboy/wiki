Welcome to the Aadenboy Wiki! This wiki serves as an encyclopedia for everything related to me—my life, my creations, and any projects or related articles that fit here. It's also my official blogsite, where I share thoughts, updates, and just whatever I want to talk about.

About the wiki itself

The Aadenboy Wiki is built with MediaWiki. I edit it on my end, then build and push it to the GitHub repo. While it is technically a locked wiki (only I can edit it), anyone can send suggestions and corrections to me, so the wiki aspect is still somewhat retained.

The wiki will contain articles about stuff directly (and publically) related to me, alongside any of my creations, and projects I have contributed to. All information presented on the wiki should be verifiable, as I specifically edit them in.

About blogposts

The wiki—while somewhat out of the nature of what a wiki is—also hosts any blogposts I make. The term "blogpost" is being used pretty broadly here, as it may—more often than not—contain content that isn't necessarily a blogpost, but I feel like sharing nonetheless.
