Anna is a white monopedal cag. Her eyes are similar to Linda's and her tail is comparable to Miguel's. She possesses the same paws as her brother Kyle.

Personality

Anna is typically seen as calm and friendly towards those she meets, but amongst those she knows better, she becomes more rambunctious and generally critterish. Her true self tends to be sillier, if not sassier, but she handles it with ease in regards to others. She will often have her tongue stuck out when making expressions or when focused on a task, especially if physical or mentally challenging; she is aware of it and prefers to do it.

She has a keen sense of strategy and is hard to outwit.

Characteristics

She's very soft, but this usually isn't presently shown.

Her speech bubble is a simple connector.
