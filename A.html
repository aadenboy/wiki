A is a monopedal cag persona of Aaden. He is a slightly reddish-brown in color with bright beige fur at the front of the torso and face, at his paws, and at the tip of his tail. He wears square-framed glasses.

Personality

A is the same as Aaden.

Characteristics

While dominantly a cag, he can become any other species as he chooses (alongside other forms of cag), though the state is temporary.

His speech bubble is the same as Aaden's.
