Daryl is an emerald-colored bipedal cag. Daryl has tufts of fur between his ears, on his chest, and at his tail. He also commonly wears sunglasses.

Personality

Daryl is casual and cultured. He is most often seen exploring various places (an interest brought about by George), and surfing with Dan. He doesn't mind joining in with whatever activity is going on at the moment, and can slide into groups very easily. He is highly immersed in internet culture.

Characteristics

Daryl's sunglasses are assistive equipment as his eyes are extremely sensitive to light. Without them on, his vision is incredibly washed out and saturated with white glowing spots, making discerning most objects impossible. His sunglasses are extremely dimmed, letting little light pass through. For the same reason, he has uncanny clarity in extremely dark environments.

His speech bubble is solely his dialogue, which is written in Arial using the same color as his fur and an additional outline surrounding the text.
