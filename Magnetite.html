Magnetite (Fe3O4, Fe2+Fe3+2O4, Lodestone, Iron(II,III) oxide, Ferrous-ferric oxide, Black rust) is a ferrimagnetic Iron oxide. His head consists of several octahedral formations, with the ears, hands, and neck tufts being a similar shape. His fur droops into a triangle shape. His overall body structure is angular and slightly rounded. He wears a magnet necklace similar to Neodymium's.

Personality

Magnetite gets along well with most cattos, and has a liking towards other Iron-based compounds due to his use as a protective coating against Oxygen, of whom he dislikes and tries to protect others against. Magnetite has mixed feelings about Water, as while he can help purify her and she constitutes their mixture together as Ferrofluid, she can also corrode other metal compounds. He is also a great navigator and often guide for others.[1] He detests Rust. He is on good terms with Ferric oxide.

Despite being a magnetic mineral, he is on better terms with Neodymium than Iron is.[2] He enjoys listening to and archiving music in addition to hiking and rock climbing.[1]
