Sulfur-36 (36S) is a rare isotope of Sulfur. She has the same characteristics of Sulfur. She wears a straw hat, with her neck tuft and the lower portion of her torso darker in color. Her torso also has vertical lines down it, with ruler markings at the rim of the darker section. A crosshair is visible around her right eye.

Personality

Sulfur-36 is a sheriff, wielding a revolver and being a great sharpshooter.[1] She similarly has a keen ability to keep track of and locate other cattos (most effectively with Sulfur and her other isotopes). In her spare time, she enjoys gardening plants like onion and garlic.
