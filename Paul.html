Paul is a pitch black quadrupedal cag. His form is entirely black, with a thick fog obscuring the space around him.

Personality

Despite his appearance, Paul is very patient, if not a little awkward. Due to being mute, he's learning to speak via MSL, and likes to listen to Linda and Chester talk about their hobbies.

Characteristics

Paul is mute, due to not having a mouth, similar to Mark. He is still able to ingest and breathe normally as if he did have one. The fog is only a visual haze, and does not linger or affect any nearby objects.

Style guide

When drawing Paul, you only need to worry about his silhouette and the fog. There should be no distinction between what part of his silhouette is which limb (if anything, lean into the ambiguity). The fog should only outline his form, and may be whatever texture you prefer.
