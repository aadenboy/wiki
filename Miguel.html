Miguel is a gold-colored monopedal cag with floating eyes, and is the little brother to Jane. His body is small and short, with a thick straight tail. Notably, his eyes are displaced away from his face, making them float and exposing them.

Personality

Miguel is an immature jerk and often standoffish to what others say, though he's only at most a nuisance. He often chooses to poke at others' abilities and is hard-set on his beliefs and ideals. While he does love his sister Jane, he finds her annoying to be around. Oscar is the only other person he mildly tolerates.

He intentionally taints his purity to be rated at a lower karat, making him less valuable. He tends to react violently if threatened anyways.

Characteristics

Like Jane, Miguel's hands (when present) float independently of his body, and thus the same rules apply here. Adding onto this, his eyes are offset forwards from his face by about the width of his hand, though he still possesses the ability to lid and close them. His eyes seem "rubber-banded" to their position in front of the head, though it does not take much effort to remove them, simply causing them to go inert until brought back into position. Since his eyes only need to be near the correct location to be "reattached", they can float past objects, and simply passing over them will attract them.

His body is made of pure gold rated at 12–18 karat, however cannot be regenerated.

His speech bubble is the simple ball-and-stick model, with the bubble only being outlines on opposite corners. His handwriting is irregular and narrow, being slightly skewed to the right.

Style guide

His mouth should not be drawn, and his eyes should be portrayed as small black spheres. His torso should be a little more rounded than other cags with a monopedal body.
