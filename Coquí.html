Coquí, pronounced /koˈki/, is a black tuxedo house cat with orange eyes. He is named after the Puerto Rican frog of the same name, though also has the nickname "Coquito" (/koˈkito/). Coquí, unlike cags, does possess a more cat-like body and traits, and may be portrayed as a regular cat with some amount of humanoid traits. He is shown to speak Spanish rather than English.
