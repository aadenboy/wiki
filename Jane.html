Jane is a primarily bipedal cag with floating limbs, being the older sister of Miguel and partner of Oscar. She is a dark orange in color, and her tail is large. Her limbs and her tail are depicted as separate from the body.

Personality

Jane is a witty, proactive individual. She loves to tease Miguel.

Characteristics

Her body is built Rayman-style, with the limbs and tail floating separately from the main body. Because of this, she can freely "detach" and "reattach" limbs from their positions in space, and objects can go inbetween each part. Having her body parts float requires effort, which can exhaust her over time, and she has a limited range of influence. She can choose to make her head float, but often doesn't.

Her speech bubble is the simple ball-and-stick model, being similar to Damian's. The connector is a triangle which connects to a rectangular bubble, the whole thing colored her fur color. Her handwriting is wide, rounded, and somewhat incomplete, being usually made of only single strokes.
