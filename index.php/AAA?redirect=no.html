AAA (read as "Triple-A") is a polypedal taur-like cat based on the concept of sparklefurs. His body is heavily patterned, with each pair of limbs having a different tiling and set of four hues. His body bears several arrow motifs, with the tail tip, ear tips, whiskers, paw pads, tongue ridge, and back fur being arrows. His underbelly, back arrow, mouth, tongue, and tail are pure white.

Personality

AAA, outside of being a personification of Aaden, is generally practical, very down-to-earth, and straight-to-the-point. He likes to point at, poke, and lick things. He loves arrows, but finds difficulty explaining why he does.

He loves his oddly squishy body, especially his arrows, taking pride in his form.

Characteristics

AAA's limbs may function as arms or legs, depending on his current pedality. In due part to his goal, his limbs, tail, whiskers, and tongue are all able to stretch infinitely with unlimited dexterity, often to point in a particular direction, his tail and whiskers getting the most practical use. He is able to create arrows out of thin air. He tastes like "battery acid spaghetti".

His body, while seemingly firm and solid to the touch, is extremely squishy and liquid-like, allowing him to melt, reform, and enter gaps and spaces he otherwise can't fit into. He is particularly springy in this regard. He also therefore lacks any organs, though can be theorized to be filled with additional arrows or a custard textured liquid. He can be rearranged and disassembled with enough effort, the most easy of which is with his face, though the effect is only temporary.

His speech bubble is the ball-and-stick model, with the stick being a thick arrow. The shape of the arrow can change depending on the relationship, usually either pointing to him or the bubble. His typeface is Arial.

Style guide

The official way to draw his patterns is to respect the form of his body, meaning that they must curve around the limbs. A Chowder-like effect is of course allowed, but not preferred. The hair is the only exception. The exact shape and hues of all of the patterns must be preserved, excluding the hind legs, which may vary.

Each pattern is:

  • A square grid.
  • A hexagonal grid.
  • A square grid with edges removed to form zig-zags.
  • A hexagonal grid with offset rows.

All of the arrows must be present on the body. The digitigrade form is preferred, but not necessary. You may shuffle the hues as you wish, so long as they aren't random and remain in distinct groups.
