Dan is a small, gray quadrupedal cag, and brothers with Kim. He has blueish gray fur, with two spiked tufts on both of his cheeks.

Personality

Dan is friendly and usually quiet, being easily agreeable. Despite being brothers with Kim, he much more enjoys surfing with Daryl rather than cooking in any manner, partly due to his size. Regardless, he won't hesitate to test out and curate what Kim cooks.

Characteristics

His speech bubble is the simple box-and-stick model, with only the corners of the box having an outline, similar to Japanese kagikakko (「」)—the same as Kim's. His handwriting is neat and compact.
