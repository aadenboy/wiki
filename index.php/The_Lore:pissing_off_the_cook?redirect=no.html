pissing off the cook is a comic in The Lore featuring aa and Liam, who place themselves into jars to mess with Kim.

Transcription

An unlabeled brown container and a glass jar containing aa are seen in a metallic cabinet. Kim opens the door to check inside, spotting aa.

aa: you sh—
Kim: (with shocks) get out. aa: cmoon

Kim begins emitting a bit of electricity from his head, as aa is not supposed to be there. He grabs the jar and tilts it back a bit.

Kim: how would i even...
aa: mint
Kim: (with shocks) ...no

He opens the container, dumping aa onto the floor. The other container rattles as something makes a stifled chuckle. Kim's ear perks up at the sound. Opening the jar, it is revealed to be Liam shoved inside, realizing he's been caught.

Liam: (sweating) h— hii

Kim doesn't say anything, though the shocks emitting from his head swells greatly, causing him to become immensely pissed off. aa, who was now sitting on the floor, is scooped back up by Kim and dumped into the container holding Liam, making both yelp. Kim then vigorously shakes the container while the shocks intensify, making Liam laugh and aa turn to yelling.

Commentary

I think this one's fairly obvious: Kim doesn't want either of them messing around in his kitchen. Considering aa is newer, I think Kim would at least tolerate him somewhat, though his actions do mildly trigger him. Liam, on the other hand, is definitely a repeat offender. Though, if Kim was able to be rational here he wouldn't resort to mixing them togther.

Had I mentioned that aa and Liam are good friends? No? I want to mess around with Liam, hehe.
