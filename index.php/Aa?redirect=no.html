aa (pronounced /æ~ɐ~a~ɑ/, /eɪ eɪ/, or "Double-A") is a semiliquid omnipedal cag personification of Aaden. His overall body is made of a teal liquid-like substance that has glowing swirls of tealish-green throughout it. His "fur" droops down at the cheeks, and is puffed below the neck and at and down the end of the tail. His paws are spherical, and his eyes are usually blank white.

Personality

aa is an alternate persona used by Aaden, primarily characterized with the cag being overall rambunctious, silly, and outwards. Beyond that, he acts mostly similarly to Aaden.

Characteristics

aa's entire body is composed of a semiliquid substance which ebbs and flows. The liquid itself typically does not track or drip anywhere, but he can melt or solidify at will. His body is described to taste like chocolate mint and spearmint, though the actual physical properties of the liquid is largely unknown. He is able to change to any size without much difficulty, and will exert extreme outward pressure if blocked. He also glows.

His body can change to any posture as needed, though this is typically scoped to having multiple legs in a quadrupedal-like stance, being bipedal, or being taur-like. His limbs, despite lacking any digits, can still grab and interact with items just fine, possibly due to it being a liquid, however he is limited in the gestures he can make. He cannot feel any pain, and often reacts positively to such antics.

His eyes are usually solid white, making his facing direction ambiguous (presumed forward), however he can choose at will to gain pupils, often for effect or emotion. His tail is also generally fluffy in any pattern, but can change into the rough shape of a four-digit paw, allowing slight grip without any additional dexterity.

His speech bubble is a simple connector, and the typeface is simply Aaden's existing handwriting, without much accomodation for readability.

Style guide

The typical way to make his body pattern is to take an existing pattern like a grid of lines and smudge or distort it. While large areas of green are fine, there should generally be more blue than green overall. If you don't have the ability to do such, you can instead draw them manually or use an existing liquid swirl/tie-dye texture.

He is a liquid, and should not be implied to have fur. You can assume that the "tufts" of "fur" at his cheek, neck, and tail are simply spherical or metaball-like protrusions. His head should be a sphere and his legs should be cylinders, rather than having a feline structure. His paws are spheres and should not have any digits or paw pads, and generally retain their spherical (or at least rounded) form even when used to stand on the ground.
