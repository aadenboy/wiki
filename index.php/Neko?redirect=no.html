Neko

Gender

Agender (it/its)

Author

Aaden

Pedality

Quadrupedal

Neko is a brown cat-like creature representing the kanji "'neko'" (猫). Its body is composed of kanji characters representing the side that it is on.

Personality

Neko is a confident, well-mannered individual, but can easily come off as a heavily excited tourist.

It does not like when anything goes inside of it or between its kanjis, and will attempt to harm the perpetrator.

Characteristics

Neko's entire body is composed of kanji that translate to the part of the body it represents. The face and tail are the character "猫", translating to "cat", and the eyes, mouth, tongue, and fangs are each the characters "目", "口", "舌", "牙", respectively. Its sides are "側", its back "背", the head "頭", and the bottom "足". The is no internal structure and most of the strokes float on their own. Its feet form from the bottom-most portions of the "側" kanjis.
