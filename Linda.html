Linda is a pink quadrupedal cag, and partners with Chester. Her legs are like tips, with arches connecting between them. Her tail is straight and somewhat thick. Her eyes appear lidded.

Personality

Linda is a calm and collected person, being soft casually but stern when necessary. She strives on acting as a guiding force, and will carefully listen to others She primarily enjoys painting and drawing digitally, usually of landscapes or anyone she meets, and has taught Sauce how to draw.

Characteristics

Her legs are based upon a doodle in which they are drawn by simply drawing arches between each foot. Due to the structure, she does not have any actual "feet" and is unable to properly perform functions which would require digits like grabbing with one hand.

Her whiskers are prosthetics and can be removed and reattached as needed.

Her speech bubble is the common ball-and-stick model, with the bubble having no outline and the connector being disconnected from the bubble itself. Her handwriting is tidy.

Style guide

When drawing her legs, they should be thinnest at the tip, with no discernible paw region. The area between the legs should be a smooth arc, with no straight portions. Bending the arc will be necessary for certain poses.
