Omnipedal; often quadrupedal

Acquaintances

George, Greg

Doug is a shapeshifter which has the appearance of a cat. He is always seen as a stock image of a real life cat, and is slightly transparent.

Personality

Doug is elusive and not much is known about him, due to his absence since his initial appearance. He has relations to George and Greg.

Characteristics

He does not possess the ability to speak. The transition between forms is instantaneous, and is dependent on how he is viewed; multiple angles may use different images, and he may switch between images as he moves. Due to his original purpose being as a reference image, he is visible through objects as if he were always in front, though is visually transparent.
